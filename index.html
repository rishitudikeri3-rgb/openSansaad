How it works

Deterministic code for mechanical work; language-model agents only where judgment is needed, and every agent answer is checked before it is kept.

1 · Corpus

Primary sources

57 documents from e-Gazette, sansad.in, the Parliament Digital Library, PRS and the Law Commission: the 1996 Act as passed, the three amending Acts, their Bills, Ordinances and the Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha debates. Rate-limited, cached, every file hashed.

2 · Parser

Clause trees and operations

The 1996 text becomes 553 addressable clauses (s34.ss2.cl(b)). Rules read the amending Acts into 105 insert / substitute / omit operations: 94 resolved, 11 left for the agents.

3 · Genealogy Agent

Maps what the rules cannot

Decides which old clause each new one descends from when an amendment renumbers or splits provisions. It chooses only among candidates the parser built.

4 · Contradiction Agent

Revises its own output

Checks the whole lineage for inconsistencies (text that moved but was mapped elsewhere, a clause claimed twice) and sends wrong mappings back for revision.

5 · Intent Agent

Searches for the reason

Escalates: Statement of Objects and Reasons → Lok Sabha debate → Law Commission report. It may only quote.

6 · Verification Agent

Blocks unsourced claims

A claim survives only if its quote is found verbatim in a primary document, is not the Chair speaking, and a reviewer model agrees it states a reason for this amendment. Otherwise: "intent not determinable".

Results

Genealogy scored against a hand-built answer key for all 104 lineage edges of the three amending Acts; the 11 "hard" edges are the ones rules cannot decide.

The Contradiction loop matters when the first pass is the cheap model: on s.37(1) the 20B model paired clauses by their labels; the loop found new clause (b) has the text of old (a), and the revision fixed it. Caveats: one Act, no held-out data; the strong model alone also scores 6/6.

OpenSansad, an Agentic AI course project, BMS College of Engineering. Models: open-weight gpt-oss 120B / 20B on Groq's free tier; every model call is cached, so these results replay offline. Work in progress: commencement dates per provision, court judgments (s.87 struck down, 2019) and more Acts are not yet in the lineage.
